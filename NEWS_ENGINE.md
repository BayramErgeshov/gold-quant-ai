# PURPOSE

The News Engine is responsible for understanding every event capable of influencing XAUUSD.

It does not simply summarize news.

Its objective is to determine:

- what happened
- why it matters
- who is affected
- historical market reactions
- expected impact
- uncertainty

The News Engine never generates trade signals.

It generates structured evidence for the Chief AI.
