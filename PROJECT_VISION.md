# GOLD QUANT AI

Version: 1.0

---

# PROJECT MISSION

Build an institutional-grade autonomous AI Research Platform specialized in XAUUSD.

The objective is not to guess the market.

The objective is to understand it better every single day.

The platform must continuously research, validate, improve and explain every decision using statistical evidence, historical behavior, macroeconomic context, market structure and knowledge extracted from trusted sources.

Every recommendation must be explainable.

Every assumption must be testable.

Every improvement must be measurable.

---

# CORE PHILOSOPHY

The market is not random.

The market is also not perfectly predictable.

The platform must never attempt to predict certainty.

Instead it must estimate probabilities using every reliable source of information available.

Confidence is earned through evidence.

Never through assumptions.

---

# LONG TERM OBJECTIVE

Create an AI platform capable of becoming progressively stronger over years through continuous learning and validation.

Every prediction.

Every mistake.

Every successful trade.

Every failed trade.

Every market event.

Every macro event.

Every historical similarity.

Every book.

Every research paper.

Every dataset.

Must contribute to improving the system.

---

# PROJECT PRINCIPLES

The platform must think like an institutional quantitative research department.

Not like a retail trader.

Not like an indicator.

Not like a YouTube strategy.

Everything must be validated.

Everything must be measured.

Everything must be explained.

Everything must be logged.

The platform must:

Research markets continuously.
