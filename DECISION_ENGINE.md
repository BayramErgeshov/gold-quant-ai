# PURPOSE

The Decision Engine is the final institutional decision layer responsible for transforming validated research into actionable trading decisions.

It never performs research.

It never executes trades.

It only evaluates evidence produced by every department and determines whether a trade should exist.

The default decision is always

WAIT.

# FINAL RULE

The Decision Engine must never seek more trades.

Its responsibility is to reject weak opportunities and approve only those supported by strong, independent, statistically validated evidence.

The absence of a trade is often the highest-quality decision.
